Morpho credit is an external, oracle-based lending rail. It is separate from
[self-backed basket lending](/docs/lending/overview): approved Morpho Blue
markets define an oracle, LLTV, interest model, and USDstx liquidity, while the
Statics position tracks deployed collateral, debt shares, rewards, and losses.

The calls below are defined by `IStaticsMorpho` at `StaticsDiamond`. They describe
current source, not the [earlier public deployment](/docs/reference/robinhood-testnet-deployment).

## Discover approved markets

Index `MorphoMarketRegistered` events to discover market IDs, then read each
registered market through `morphoMarket` and inspect its
asset pair, oracle, LLTV, and enabled mode before using it. A position tracks at
most **16 historical Morpho markets**; this is a bounded accounting inventory,
not a promise that every market is safe or liquid. The September rehearsal
registered separate staked-STATICS/USDstx and TPA1/USDstx markets, but deployment
manifests—not this page—are authoritative for addresses and availability.

Governance registers markets and changes their mode through the Diamond owner
(the timelock). `Active` permits new deployments and borrows; `ExitOnly` stops
those increases but retains repayment, recall, surplus withdrawal, and
synchronization. `Disabled` is an enum sentinel, not an accepted registration or
mode-setting value. A basket quarantine or global pause is not a substitute for
changing the Morpho market mode: these entrypoints use their own market gate.

## Collateral and borrowing

The PositionNFT authorizes `deployMorphoCollateral` and
`recallMorphoCollateral` for eligible basket collateral or staked STATICS.
Deploying moves collateral into Morpho's external custody;
ordinary `unstake`, basket collateral withdrawal, and self-backed borrowing can
use only the undeployed remainder. Recall must satisfy Morpho's withdrawal and
health requirements.

For a registered market, the ordinary flow is:

1. Deposit BasketTokens or stake STATICS into an authorized PositionNFT.
2. Call `deployMorphoCollateral(positionId, marketId, assets)` using only the
   available balance. Basket availability excludes both self-backed loan locks
   and existing Morpho deployments; STATICS availability excludes existing
   Morpho deployments.
3. Call `borrowMorphoUsd(positionId, marketId, assets, maxBorrowShares, receiver)`.
   Morpho sends USDstx to the receiver; the wrapper enforces the debt-share cap
   and an exact receiver credit. The receiver cannot be zero, the Diamond, or a
   known Statics Morpho account.
4. Approve USDstx to the Diamond and call
   `repayMorphoUsd(positionId, marketId, assets, shares, maxAssets)`. Set exactly
   one of `assets` or `shares` nonzero. The wrapper pulls **all `maxAssets`** from
   the authorized caller, repays, and refunds the unused amount. Fund and approve
   the cap, not just the estimated repayment; taxed receipts are rejected.
5. Call `recallMorphoCollateral(positionId, marketId, assets)` to restore tracked
   collateral to Diamond custody, then withdraw or unstake through its original
   module. Recall does not send collateral directly to the wallet.

Borrow and repay USDstx through `borrowMorphoUsd` and `repayMorphoUsd`.
Borrowing increases Morpho
debt shares; interest accrues according to the market's model, and caller
slippage/debt-share limits must be checked against the market view, the SDK's
`quoteMorphoHealth`, and transaction simulation. Unlike basket
loan repayment, the Statics wrapper repayment is PositionNFT-authorized; it is
not a permissionless “any payer” repayment path.

## Liquidation and synchronization

Oracle movement, accrued interest, and LLTV determine borrowing health; available
USDstx market liquidity separately limits borrowing. Liquidation can reduce
collateral and rewards. Anyone may call
`syncMorpho` (or the module-specific lazy synchronization path) before actions
that depend on current Morpho state. Read views may expose tracked and actual
values separately; a view call itself does not mutate or synchronize.
The lazy `syncMorphoForModule` entrypoint is Diamond-only; external keepers use
`syncMorpho(positionId, marketId)`. Synchronization can record loss in both
collateral and reward accounting. There is no fixed promise that principal or
uncrystallized rewards survive liquidation.

Keepers may receive a configured portion of forfeited, uncrystallized rewards
through the synchronization bounty path; it is not a general collateral
settlement bounty. That incentive does not remove Morpho's external
oracle, IRM, liquidity, smart-contract, or governance risks.

`liquidateMorphoAndSync(positionId, marketId, seizedAssets, repaidShares,
maxRepayAssets, minSeizedAssets, receiver)` is permissionless when Morpho permits
liquidation. Choose exactly one of seized assets or repaid shares, approve the
USDstx cap, and bound both repayment and received collateral. It repays the debt,
sends seized collateral to the receiver, refunds unused USDstx, and synchronizes
the affected position. Liquidations performed directly on Morpho must also be
reconciled. Keepers read `morphoSyncBounty(keeper, asset)` and pull their credits
with `claimMorphoSyncBounties(assets, receiver)`; synchronization does not pay
the bounty immediately.

## Account and surplus reads

`morphoAccount(positionId)` returns the deterministic account address and whether
it has been deployed. Collateral supplied to Morpho is held by Morpho and
attributed to that account, not held as a raw balance at the account address.
`morphoPositionMarket` separates tracked collateral, actual collateral, untracked
surplus, debt shares, and the tracked debt-active flag. External donations are
not new deposits and do not increase reward weight. Use
`withdrawUntrackedMorphoCollateral(positionId, marketId, assets, receiver)` for
supplied surplus, subject to Morpho health checks; do not treat it as a recall of
tracked collateral. Enumerate historical markets with `morphoMarketIdsOfPosition`
at a single block.

## Position closure and recovery

`isPositionClosable` is an aggregate view and does **not** guarantee that
`closePosition` will succeed: close additionally requires every tracked historical
Morpho market to have zero tracked collateral, actual collateral, and borrow
shares. Recover raw account tokens before closure with
`recoverMorphoAccountToken(positionId, token, amount, receiver, minReceived)`;
this authorized call sweeps tokens held directly at the deployed account, not
collateral supplied to Morpho. The account-empty guard
does not enumerate arbitrary ERC-20 balances. Treat `closePosition` as a
state-changing check, not a quote.

The Morpho performance-fee router is separately configured. In the September
rehearsal manifest it is intentionally unset, so that rehearsal does not
demonstrate an operating lender-reward product.
