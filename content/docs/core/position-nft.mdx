One PositionNFT can own several independent legs at once. The ERC-721
collection is named `Statics Position` with symbol `STXPOS`, and the contract
lives at the `StaticsDiamond` address.

## What a position owns

A single PositionNFT may bundle any of the following legs:

- a global staking balance and accrued multi-asset reward claims;
- deposited and locked BasketTokens used as lending collateral;
- independent basket loan tranches;
- Morpho account control, deployed collateral, and external debt obligations;
- immediately consumable Statics Dollar Risk Share liquidity, funded incentives,
  fill proceeds, and series-migration credits;
- pairing-vault state; and
- voluntarily custodied full-range v4 LP NFTs and their hook-fee claims.

A position may also be linked to one registered Genesis NFT. The link applies
the Genesis activation multiplier to the position's global STATICS reward weight
and creates a mutual transfer lock while it exists. Direct Genesis rewards,
Genesis registration, and Genesis ownership remain separate ledger state.

ERC-721 ownership and approvals authorize every attached leg. Transferring the
NFT transfers staking balance, reward claims, collateral, obligations, and
control of voluntarily custodied LP NFTs in one move. An actively linked
Genesis NFT cannot be transferred until it is unlinked; approvals do not bypass
that lock. Externally held Uniswap v4 NFTs are not PositionNFT legs and do not
move with a transfer.

:::warning
Integrators must inspect every active leg before accepting a PositionNFT
transfer. A position carries obligations (loans, Dollar legs) as well as assets.
:::

## Creation and reuse

Opening a PositionNFT requires the exact native amount returned by
`positionCreationFee()`. The testnet fee is `0.001 ETH`. The Diamond owner may
change the raw amount without an artificial cap, and zero means free creation.
Direct creation and every atomic create-and-use path enforce the same fee and
forward it immediately, in full, to the canonical treasury — the Diamond never
retains it.

Adding legs, collateral, stake, liquidity, or debt to an existing position does
not pay the fee again. Closing a position and opening another creates a new NFT
and pays the then-current fee.

`closePosition` succeeds only after all balances, claims, collateral, loans,
Dollar legs, custodied LP NFTs, and LP claims are empty.

`isPositionClosable` is an aggregate precheck, not a guarantee: closure also
requires tracked Morpho markets to be empty. Recover raw tokens from a Morpho
account before closing because the account-empty check does not enumerate
arbitrary ERC-20 balances.

## Modular reporting interface

`StaticsDiamond` implements the pre-ERC Modular Position NFT reporting interface
(`0x212b8e93`):

- `positionState` reports current existence, a structural nonce, active-leg
  count, and unresolved-loan-obligation count;
- `isLegActive` provides constant-time membership; and
- `isPositionClosable` applies every aggregate check plus the
  mint-initialization guard.

The nonce starts at one on mint and increments for Leg attachment, Leg
detachment, loan-obligation changes, and Closure. It does not change for
transfer, approval, reward settlement, or loan extension.

## Owner index

An owner index tracks every position by its current owner
across mint, transfer, and burn. It is ERC-165 advertised through
`IPositionOwnerIndex` (`0x7ef5913d`).

| Method | Purpose |
| --- | --- |
| `positionCount(owner)` | Number of positions currently owned |
| `positionsOfOwner(owner, cursor, limit)` | Bounded pagination returning `(positionIds, nextCursor)` |
| `syncPositionOwnerIndex(positionId)` | Permissionless migration of a single legacy position into the index |

Transfers and burns remove entries with swap-and-pop; ordering is not stable.
Explicit `syncPositionOwnerIndex` calls emit `PositionOwnerIndexSynced`; ordinary
mint, transfer, and burn maintain the index alongside ERC-721 `Transfer` events.
Current source does not emit ERC-4906 metadata-update events. Migration removes
only the requested position, leaving
sibling positions indexed. The index lets integrators enumerate a wallet's
positions without looping transfer events.

## Portfolio enumeration

`IStaticsPositionPortfolio` provides `positionPortfolioCounts` and bounded,
block-consistent pages through `basketIdsOfPosition`, `loanIdsOfPosition`,
`liquidityPositionIdsOfPosition`, `globalRewardAssetsOfPosition`,
`riskSeriesIdsOfPosition`, and `morphoMarketIdsOfPosition`. Each page accepts a
cursor and a limit from **1 to 100** entries. Ordering can change after state updates;
read all pages at one block and do not treat any list as append-only.

Counts include a separate `morphoMarketCount`. The current pinned SDK's portfolio
ABI omits that field; see the [SDK compatibility warning](/docs/reference/sdk#match-the-sdk-to-the-target)
before decoding portfolio results.

## Onchain metadata

Each valid PositionNFT exposes fully onchain metadata through `tokenURI`. Current
source builds the Base64 JSON and SVG directly through `LibPositionSVG`; the
image is the Statics logo plus the position ID. There is no avatar seed,
attributes array, replaceable collection renderer, `setPositionRenderer`, or
empty-URI clearing path in this revision. Renderer addresses are historical
deployment details only when a deployment manifest explicitly lists them.

## Reward eligibility at a glance

| Reward rail | Weight | Eligibility | Exit behavior |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Basket position rewards | Deposited BasketTokens in one basket | First deposits mature at the next hourly bucket at least 24 hours later (24–25h); top-ups use a weighted pending age while matured weight remains eligible | Withdrawal removes pending shares first; locked loan collateral stays eligible |
| Global Statics rewards | Matured selected STATICS stake times its active reward multiplier | Fresh stake and selections mature at an hourly boundary 24–25 hours later; top-ups merge by weighted pending age | Undeployed stake has no cooldown; stake committed as Morpho collateral must be recalled first |
| Protocol-pool LP rewards | Full-range basket-canonical or general-pool liquidity in a custodied v4 NFT | Initial and increased deltas activate next block | NFT can leave immediately in any lifecycle state; claims stay attached |
| Dollar Risk liquidity | Supplied series Risk Shares | Staking makes shares immediately consumable | Unconsumed effective shares remain withdrawable |
| Genesis boost | Activation multiplier on a linked Genesis NFT | Link and activation state determine effective global STATICS weight | Credit recovery clears only the Genesis boost and link; the PositionNFT and unrelated legs remain |
