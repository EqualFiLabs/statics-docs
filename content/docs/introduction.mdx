## What Statics does

Statics is **post-launch financial infrastructure**, not a launchpad.

Launchpads help tokens launch. Statics helps make onchain assets useful afterward: redeemable multi-asset baskets, connected trading pools, self-backed credit, fee sharing, permanent protocol-owned liquidity, and a senior/junior Statics Dollar system.

> Statics turns onchain assets into redeemable financial markets with connected liquidity, arbitrage paths, fee earning, and self-backed credit. It begins with curated baskets and can expand into utility infrastructure for token projects before open creation is enabled.

Volume, fees, rewards, and liquidity growth are **possible outcomes of real activity**, not guarantees.

## How a basket works

A **static basket** is a fixed bundle of one to sixteen ERC-20 constituents. Each basket has its own BasketToken:

- **Mint** — deposit the fixed constituent bundle (plus any mint fee) and receive BasketTokens.
- **Redeem** — burn BasketTokens and receive the same bundle back (less any redemption fee).
- **Trade** — swap BasketToken against each constituent on canonical Uniswap v4 pools created at launch.
- **Hold productively** — deposit BasketTokens into a PositionNFT to earn routed basket fee shares and to borrow self-backed credit.

Baskets are deliberately **not ERC-4626**. Historical fees do not change the conversion rate, and new minters do not buy accrued yield. One BasketToken is always a claim on the same fixed bundle.

## Already-launched tokens

Statics is not where a token has to launch. After a token exists elsewhere, a project can put **token inventory or treasury capital** to work by seeding a basket and its pools (when creation access allows).

A basket can add:

- redeemable multi-asset utility;
- connected canonical liquidity;
- additional mint/redeem and pool paths that arbitrageurs may use;
- fee-earning participation for depositors, LPs, and stakers;
- self-backed constituent credit against deposited BasketTokens.

:::note Creator economics
Creation records an immutable creator address. The creator receives **no admin
power**, but current source assigns that address a fixed **5% of bilateral hook
fees** from its basket pools. The share is pull-based and remains claimable
after decommissioning. The permissionless general-pool source uses the same
fixed creator share. These newer economics are not yet deployed at the recorded
Robinhood testnet addresses.
:::

On the current beta, public creation is closed. Partner baskets, if any, are an operational choice while the protocol retains owner-only creation. Details: [Rollout](/docs/rollout) and [Basket creation](/docs/baskets/creation).

## Fees and permanent liquidity

When people mint, redeem, swap, borrow, flash-borrow, or use Dollar flows, the protocol can charge configured fees. Swap fees on Statics protocol pools are bilateral hook fees. A share of eligible fees can accumulate as protocol-owned inventory and **may** compound into more full-range permanent liquidity when sides match.

None of that is automatic depth: seed liquidity bootstraps markets; **activity** determines whether fees and POL grow.

## Mainnet launch and protocol rollout

The STATICS Genesis launch is live on Robinhood mainnet. It established the
fixed token supply, six-curve STATICS/WETH market, Operators collection,
backing, vesting, fee ingress, and launch rewards described in these docs.

The broader basket, Dollar, lending, and general-pool suite has its own rollout
and governance controls. Basket creation is owner-only whenever
`creationFee()` is zero and becomes open when governance sets a positive fee.
The recorded Robinhood testnet deployment remains available as a separate
integration reference; its fixture addresses are not the mainnet Genesis
deployment.

See [Rollout](/docs/rollout) for production availability and the distinction
between the live Genesis system and the basket-suite test environment.

## Risks and limitations

- **Recorded testnet software** is an integration environment: its addresses and parameters can change and are not production defaults.
- **Token compatibility** is not certified by inclusion in a basket.
- **No guaranteed** volume, arb profit, reward APY, peg, or liquidity growth.
- **Self-backed credit** is not oracle-liquidated cross-margin; read lending and recovery rules before borrowing.
- **Upgradeability** remains until a final immutability ceremony; ExitOnly is a governed software state on upgradeable diamonds.

Read the [security model](/docs/reference/security-model) before integrating or depositing value.

## Technical architecture

Statics is implemented as two coordinated EIP-2535 diamonds:

```text Statics two-diamond layout
ETH / WETH / pegged collateral
        |
        v
StaticsDollarCoreDiamond   collateral + issuance + solvency + recovery
        |
   USDstx + Risk Shares
        v
StaticsDiamond (EIP-2535)  user gateway + PositionNFT + shared custody
        |
   one pool per basket constituent
        v
Uniswap v4 PoolManager + StaticsSwapFeeHook + LiquidityManager
```

| Surface | Purpose |
| --- | --- |
| Static baskets | Fixed-bundle ERC-20 basket tokens of up to 16 assets with action-size fee tiers. |
| Statics Dollar | Senior dollar (`USDstx`) from volatile or pegged collateral, plus series-scoped Risk Shares (`ethLEV`). |
| Shared PositionNFT | One transferable ERC-721 for dollar legs, basket collateral, loans, rewards, and staked liquidity. |
| Global rewards | Stake `STATICS` and opt into up to 64 reward assets per position. |
| Self-backed lending | Borrow a basket's constituent vector against deposited basket collateral. |
| Flash composition | Borrow a basket's constituents atomically through a typed callback. |
| Canonical v4 liquidity | One zero-native-fee Uniswap v4 pool per constituent with a bilateral swap-fee hook. |
| Permanent liquidity | Matched protocol-owned inventory becomes hook-owned full-range liquidity. |

`StaticsDiamond` is the ordinary integration address, the PositionNFT contract, and the custody boundary for basket and Dollar periphery assets. `StaticsDollarCoreDiamond` is a separate custody and solvency boundary. Both are owned by the same `StaticsTimelock`.
