Statics is staged financial infrastructure for onchain assets. **Statics Genesis** established the STATICS token, Doppler market, Operators collection, and backing system on Robinhood mainnet. Statics Phase 1 adds public and permissioned Uniswap v4 markets, PositionNFT accounts, STATICS staking, rewards, range gauges, and managed protocol-owned liquidity.

The [Phase 1 launch guide](/docs/start/phase-one-launch) shows what Phase 1 includes. The [rollout](/docs/rollout) separates source readiness from a verified deployment. This site does not yet publish a verified Phase 1 mainnet protocol address.

## What Phase 1 enables

- **Create and trade public pools:** two compatible ERC-20s or native ETH with an ERC-20. The Phase 1 launch configuration sets a 0.01 ETH [creation fee](/docs/liquidity/protocol-pools), allowing anyone to create a pool with exact payment and the required creator authorization. Confirm the deployed fee before submitting a transaction.
- **Run a permissioned venue:** governance creates a pool with creator-authorized terms, a separate hook, controller, trusted routing, and approved liquidity positions. See [Permissioned pools](/docs/liquidity/permissioned-pools).
- **Provide liquidity:** earn the creator-selected native v4 LP fee, and optionally manage a v4 NFT under a [PositionNFT range gauge](/docs/liquidity/canonical-lp-rewards) for separately funded rewards.
- **Stake STATICS:** choose reward assets, pass the eligibility delay, claim accrued fees, and allocate raw stake to protocol-funded pool incentives. See [Global rewards](/docs/rewards/global-rewards).
- **Build protocol liquidity:** a public pool may activate claim-backed [managed POL](/docs/liquidity/permanent-liquidity). Activation and market activity determine whether any inventory becomes available.

A market's registration does not make its assets basket backing, Dollar collateral, or borrowable. Trading volume, rewards, arbitrage, and POL growth depend on actual funding and activity.

## What follows

Phase 2 adds fixed-composition, redeemable [baskets](/docs/baskets/overview), basket pools, self-backed [credit](/docs/lending/overview), flash composition, and protocol-side Genesis integration. Phase 3 adds the [Statics Dollar](/docs/dollar/overview) and its separate Core custody boundary. Phase 4 adds Morpho integration. These pages remain in the site so builders can study the full design, but those features are not part of Phase 1.

Baskets are deliberately not ERC-4626. A BasketToken represents a fixed vector of one to sixteen assets; its mint and redemption path appears only in Phase 2. See the [glossary](/docs/glossary) for terms and the [integration guide](/docs/reference/integration) for release-aware developer steps.

## Statics Genesis

The [Operators and Vault](/docs/genesis/overview) are part of Statics Genesis, which is already live. Genesis direct rewards and secured credit have their own contracts. Registering or linking an Operator through the Statics protocol is a later, Phase 2 integration step.
