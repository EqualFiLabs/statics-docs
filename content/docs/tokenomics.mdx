STATICS launches with a **fixed supply of 1,000,000,000 tokens**. The Genesis
launcher assigns 800 million to the six-curve Doppler market and commits the
remaining 200 million to two independent treasury-side obligations. The launch
token has no post-launch mint path in the committed configuration.

:::warning Source-committed, not yet production-ratified
These economics are fixed in the current Genesis launch source, but production
execution remains disabled while `APPROVED_ROBINHOOD_LAUNCH_CONFIG_HASH` is
`bytes32(0)`. This page does not claim that the standalone launch has executed.
:::

## Fixed supply and allocation

| Destination or commitment | STATICS | Share of supply |
| --- | ---: | ---: |
| Six-curve Doppler market inventory | 800,000,000 | 80.00% |
| Doppler-native treasury vesting | 100,100,000 | 10.01% |
| Backing for 555 treasury-vesting Operators | 99,900,000 | 9.99% |
| **Fixed total supply** | **1,000,000,000** | **100.00%** |

The 200 million treasury-side allocation is not 200 million tokens of
immediately liquid treasury inventory. Of that amount, 100.1 million follows a
zero-cliff, 60-day Doppler-native vesting schedule. The other 99.9 million moves
into `StaticsGenesisVault` to back the 555 Operators held in a separate 60-day
treasury vesting contract.

## Six launch curves

![STATICS Doppler launch curves showing six inventory bands, their approximate valuation ranges, token allocations, shares, tick ranges, and position counts.](/images/launch-curves.jpg)

The Solidity launch geometry is authoritative. It creates **56 positions**
across six curves, assigns exactly **800 million STATICS**, and gives the
permanent tail **120 million STATICS (15%)**. The growth curve ends where the
tail begins at tick `-77,800`. `FAR_TICK` is `887,100`; the tail position's
ordered upper boundary is `887,200` at tick spacing 100.

| Curve | Inventory | Supply share | Tick range | Positions |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| Launch access | 20,000,000 | 2.5% | `-168,800` to `-153,800` | 11 |
| Genesis discovery | 60,000,000 | 7.5% | `-160,700` to `-139,900` | 11 |
| Early market | 100,000,000 | 12.5% | `-146,900` to `-123,800` | 11 |
| Core market | 160,000,000 | 20.0% | `-130,800` to `-100,800` | 11 |
| Growth market | 340,000,000 | 42.5% | `-107,700` to `-77,800` | 11 |
| Permanent tail | 120,000,000 | 15.0% | `-77,800` to `887,200` | 1 |
| **Total** | **800,000,000** | **100% of launch inventory** |  | **56** |

The curves overlap intentionally. They are separate liquidity distributions,
not six consecutive price buckets.

## Operator backing

The fixed **5,555 STATICS Operators** collection begins in two custody groups:

| Operator IDs | Count | Initial custody | Backing at finalization |
| --- | ---: | --- | ---: |
| `1..5000` | 5,000 | `StaticsGenesisVault` | Added as each Operator enters circulation |
| `5001..5555` | 555 | `StaticsTreasuryVesting` | 99,900,000 STATICS |

Each circulating Operator represents **180,000 STATICS of gross backing**.
Therefore the 555 treasury-vesting Operators require exactly 99.9 million
STATICS at finalization. Vault-owned Operators do not require backing while
they remain in vault inventory; acquisition brings the required backing into
the vault before the Operator becomes a circulating claim.

Backing is reserve accounting, not a promise that an Operator will maintain a
particular USD value. Acquisition and redemption rules are documented in the
[Operators overview](/docs/genesis/overview).

## Vesting and market circulation

The two 60-day schedules have different assets and start times:

- **100.1 million STATICS** vest to the immutable treasury beneficiary from the
  Doppler Launch timestamp.
- **555 Operators** vest from token ID 5001 upward from the Genesis Finalize
  timestamp, with their full 99.9 million STATICS backing already held by the
  vault.

Anyone may trigger release, but the configured contracts always send the
released assets to their immutable or governed recipient. See
[Launch and vesting](/docs/genesis/launch-and-vesting) for the full Prepare,
Launch, and Finalize sequence.

## Protocol facts and market assumptions

| Machine-checkable protocol fact | Market interpretation that is not guaranteed |
| --- | --- |
| Fixed 1B STATICS supply | Future token price or fully diluted valuation |
| Exact tick ranges, shares, and 800M inventory | USD values shown for illustrative curve bands |
| 180,000 STATICS gross backing per circulating Operator | A stable USD value for that backing |
| Exact vesting principal and duration | Market liquidity when tokens vest |
| Deterministic token/WETH geometry from a specified pool state | ETH/USD at launch, volume, or arbitrage participation |

The approximate valuation labels in the curve image are an **economic model**,
not an EVM guarantee or price forecast. Their USD meaning changes with WETH/USD
and live pool state. Formal verification can prove the encoded allocation and
geometry; launch-time price attestations and future market behavior remain
separate evidence.
