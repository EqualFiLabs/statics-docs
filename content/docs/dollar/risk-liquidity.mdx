Dollar Risk liquidity is a series-scoped position leg, not ordinary passive
staking. A supplier deposits Risk Shares for a volatile Dollar series and makes
them available for consumption by the protocol's senior/junior settlement
flows. Consumption reduces effective shares; the original deposit is not a
guaranteed withdrawable balance. The interface is `IStaticsDollarRiskLiquidity`
at `StaticsDiamond`.

## Supply and withdraw

Approve the ERC-1155 Risk Share token with `setApprovalForAll(StaticsDiamond,
true)`, then choose the path that fits
the position lifecycle:

```text
createAndStakeRiskShares(seriesId, amount, receiver) payable
stakeRiskShares(positionId, seriesId, amount)
riskLiquidity(positionId, seriesId) -> effective shares and accrued proceeds
unstakeRiskShares(positionId, seriesId, amount, receiver)
```

`createAndStakeRiskShares` creates the PositionNFT and attaches the liquidity in
one action and requires the exact current `positionCreationFee()` in native
currency. `stakeRiskShares` adds to an existing authorized position. Both pull
Risk Shares from the caller and require an active series. Unstake requires
PositionNFT authorization and returns `principalOut`, which can differ from the
requested amount because of share rounding. Only remaining effective shares in
the current book epoch can be removed; an old-epoch leg cannot withdraw consumed
principal. Accrued proceeds remain separate claims. Read `riskLiquidity` at the
current block and simulate before withdrawal.

## Claim proceeds

Use `claimRiskProceeds(positionId, seriesId, receiver)` after reading the accrued
amount; it returns collateral, USDstx, and STATICS amounts and has no
per-asset-minimum parameters. It requires PositionNFT authorization and settles
the leg before paying; a claim with no proceeds reverts with `NoRiskProceeds`.
Proceeds are tracked across the series lifecycle and can include the three
allowed incentive assets: the series collateral, `USDstx`, and the configured
staking token. Claims settle the position's accrued proceeds; they are distinct
from global STATICS reward allocations and do not earn ordinary passive
mint/recombination-fee allocations.

## Fund incentives and finalize

`IStaticsDollarRiskIncentives` exposes the funding and finalization surface at
the same Diamond. Funders approve the relevant ERC-20 to the Diamond and call
one of the permissionless paths
`fundRiskCollateralIncentives(seriesId, amount)`,
`fundRiskDollarIncentives(seriesId, amount)`, or
`fundRiskStaticsIncentives(seriesId, amount)`. These accept only the series
collateral, `USDstx`, or configured STATICS respectively. Funding requires an
active series in an `Active` or `ReduceOnly` profile and credits the measured
received amount. There is no funder refund function. Incentives are released
proportionally to effective Risk liquidity consumed in fills, not simply for
elapsed staking time.

Read `riskIncentives(seriesId)` for the three reserves, token bindings,
destination, and finalized flag. Permissionless `finalizeRiskIncentives(seriesId)`
routes only **unspent incentive reserves**, under these lifecycle conditions:

| Profile and old series | Destination |
| --- | --- |
| Profile not retired; old series `Recoverable` or `Closed`; a distinct active successor exists | Successor series incentive reserves |
| Profile `Retired`; old series `Recoverable`, `Retired`, or `Closed` | Global non-swap reward/treasury accounting |

It returns `(destinationSeriesId, routedGlobal)` and returns the recorded route
on repeated finalization. Already attributed position proceeds are not swept by
this routing; claim them through `claimRiskProceeds` for their original series.

Risk liquidity inherits Dollar oracle, health, series, rollover, and successor
risks. A successful approval or funding transaction is not a promise of
principal recovery, proceeds, or a particular liquidation outcome.
