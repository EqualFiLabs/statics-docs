Statics' security model rests on three pillars: who can change the protocol
(authority), how assets are protected (custody), and what the protocol assumes
about the chain (liveness).

## Authority

- One `StaticsTimelock` owns both diamonds. All Diamond cuts, economic
  configuration, lifecycle release, hook fee configuration, and treasury or
  guardian changes flow through it after delay.
- Basket creation is owner-only while `creationFee()` is zero, and open to any
  caller paying the exact fee while it is positive. Neither state constitutes
  token certification. Current deployment status is on [Rollout](/docs/rollout).
- Diamond cuts are the sole implementation upgrade mechanism. The intended final
  release removes Diamond-cut authority from both diamonds; until then,
  timelocked upgradeability can change protocol behavior after delay.
- Reward-asset selection is a PositionNFT owner action and requires no governance
  admission.
- Current source uses a separate `poolCreationFee()` switch for general pools.
  Zero is owner-only; a positive exact fee opens creation. Creators receive the
  fixed fee share but no decommissioning or configuration authority.

## Custody

- Shared custody increases the importance of exact reservations and hostile token
  analysis. The [reservation equation](/docs/core/custody) requires the physical
  balance to cover the sum of all sub-account reservations.
- A physical reservation deficit is a black-swan incident, contained through
  fail-closed checks and guardian quarantine rather than a routine
  socialized-loss waterfall. No claimant class is intended to absorb another
  class's loss during ordinary operation.
- PositionNFT transfer moves all attached protocol rights and obligations.
- A linked Genesis NFT and PositionNFT are mutually transfer-locked. The link
  requires the same actual owner on both NFTs; ERC-721 approvals do not bypass
  the owner check.
- Genesis secured credit is post-epoch, fixed-term, and bounded by the Genesis
  backing. Additional draws and new facilities can be paused independently;
  repayment remains permissionless and may be partial. Draws cannot exceed
  restored capacity or available Vault backing. Recovery settles reward books,
  clears only the recovered Genesis boost/link, preserves the PositionNFT's
  unrelated ledger state, and returns the NFT to vault inventory.
- Initial Operator custody is split between 5,000 Vault IDs and 555 fully backed
  treasury-vesting IDs. The immutable vesting contract can release only IDs
  5001 through 5555 under the 60-day schedule; recipient administration cannot
  change timing, principal, or asset type.
- Dollar Core collateral remains outside Diamond custody reservations — only fees
  explicitly transferred to the periphery enter shared reservations.

## Liveness and oracles

- [Morpho credit](/docs/lending/morpho) adds external oracle, LLTV, interest-rate,
  liquidity, and liquidation assumptions. Supplied collateral leaves Diamond
  custody; synchronization records collateral loss and can forfeit
  uncrystallized rewards. Already deployed STATICS is not freely withdrawable.
  Market admission does not guarantee safe collateral valuation or liquidity.
  Morpho market `ExitOnly` is its own governance gate, separate from basket
  quarantine and the ordinary global pause.
- Dollar safety depends on configured oracle, sequencer, collateral, health, and
  governance parameters. A misconfigured or manipulated feed impairs or halts a
  profile; impairment latches exits until 48 continuous healthy hours are
  checkpointed.
- Protocol-pool liquidity inherits Uniswap v4, immutable-dependency,
  price-manipulation, inventory, and impermanent-loss risk.
- Hook-owned permanent liquidity is intentionally non-withdrawable while its pool
  remains active.
- General-pool registration does not admit either currency as basket backing,
  Dollar collateral, or a borrowable asset. Its spot price and liquidity are not
  protocol solvency inputs.
- No keeper runs automatically. Claims, LP activation, recovery, compounding,
  treasury distribution, and ExitOnly unwind depend on users, integrators, or
  externally motivated keepers.

## Composition and permits

- Flash callbacks expand atomic composition but not authority. Receivers must
  defend their own pools, approvals, slippage, and minimum profit. Statics
  provides no receiver allowlist, generic router, callback privilege, or fee
  exemption.
- Permit signatures authorize caller allowances, not a complete economic intent.
  Their signed value may exceed the current action input and remain reusable; a
  failed permit never substitutes another owner — the typed action still pulls
  only from `msg.sender` under ordinary allowance rules.

## Funding risks

- Permissionless Risk incentive funding is restricted to the series collateral,
  Statics Dollar, and configured staking token, but funders still bear series,
  profile, consumption, rollover, and retirement timing risk.
- [Dollar Risk liquidity](/docs/dollar/risk-liquidity) is consumed during fills.
  Incentive funders have no refund route; unspent reserves may roll into a
  successor series or route globally on retirement. Original deposits are not
  guaranteed withdrawal amounts.
- Basket-loan recovery and Dollar expired-risk recovery have distinct
  caller-incentive formulas; other basket and liquidity maintenance has no
  guaranteed caller or protocol bounty.
- Genesis-credit recovery is a third, distinct path. It becomes permissionless
  only after maturity plus grace and pays a configured share of the fixed
  recovery residual to the caller while distributing the remainder through the
  existing Genesis reward books.
- Morpho synchronization has a separate configurable keeper bounty drawn from
  forfeited uncrystallized rewards, not a fixed percentage of collateral. A
  keeper must claim the resulting per-asset credit separately.

## Testnet fixtures are not production trust assumptions

The public testnet's owner-mintable STATICS token, mock USDG and oracles, faucet,
and two-minute timelock are fixtures. This revision has not undergone an
independent third-party security review, and no review of an earlier commit
constitutes production assurance for the code deployed here.
