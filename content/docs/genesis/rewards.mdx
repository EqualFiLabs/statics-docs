Genesis rewards have two accounting phases. The temporary
`GenesisLaunchDistributor` records launch-era liabilities; after the governed
handoff, `StaticsDiamond` records permanent Genesis liabilities. The books and
custody boundaries remain separate, so handoff does not migrate historical
balances.

## Lazy accrual

While the launch distributor is active, every Genesis claim entrypoint also
harvests the authenticated Doppler beneficiary share before settling the claim.
There is no required keeper transaction between harvesting and claiming.

The permanent integration keeps the same lazy-processing principle for its
active distributor. Applications should quote and submit a claim against the
current distributor state rather than assuming rewards are already indexed.

## Owner claims

Single-token and prior-owner paths remain available:

| Call | Purpose |
| --- | --- |
| `claimGenesisRewards(genesisId, asset, receiver)` | Claim one reward asset still attributed to the held Genesis token |
| `claimGenesisOwnerRewards(asset, receiver)` | Claim crystallized credits from Genesis tokens previously transferred away |
| `claimAllGenesisRewards(genesisIds, receiver)` | Claim both categories for a caller-supplied set of owned Genesis IDs |

`claimAllGenesisRewards` pays both STATICS and WETH and harvests at most once
for the batch. The caller must own every supplied Genesis ID; the contract does
not enumerate the caller's wallet. An empty ID array is valid and claims only
prior-owner credits. Duplicate IDs do not multiply the claim.

## Treasury claims

`claimAllGenesisTreasuryRewards(receiver)` is the two-asset treasury mirror.
The receiver is explicit, but the claim remains governed by the distributor's
treasury accounting and cannot withdraw user liabilities.

Every batch updates both reward books before making external transfers and sends
each reward asset at most once. Single-asset and single-token calls remain for
wallets that prefer smaller calldata.

## Transfer and recovery boundaries

When a registered Genesis changes owners, rewards attributed through the
transfer boundary settle to the previous owner's credit book. Registration
does not need to be repeated by the new owner, while activation resets to Tier
0. A recovered Genesis follows the same owner-changing settlement boundary;
recovery also removes its direct weight and any PositionNFT boost before the
recovery residual is indexed.

Useful events for an indexer include `GenesisRegistered`, `GenesisLinked`,
`GenesisUnlinked`, `GenesisWeightChanged`, `GenesisRevenueAccrued`,
`GenesisRewardsClaimed`, `GenesisOwnerRewardsClaimed`, and
`GenesisTreasuryRewardsClaimed`. A batch can emit multiple per-token claim
events, so indexers should reconcile their sums against the call return values
and current reward views.
