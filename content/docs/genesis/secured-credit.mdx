Genesis secured credit is a separate facility from BasketToken lending. It is
available only after the Genesis Epoch and releases a bounded portion of the
fixed STATICS backing represented by a Genesis NFT. It does not use an oracle,
price-based liquidation, or a percentage fee over the released principal.

## Opening a facility

The maximum principal is 171,000 STATICS against 180,000 STATICS of gross
Operator backing. The owner calls `openGenesisCredit(genesisId, principal)` with
the exact current native origination service fee from
`quoteGenesisCredit(principal)`.

The service fee is flat, native, and intentionally unbounded. It is paid for a
secured-access term, not added to STATICS principal. The governed split is:

```text
10% -> permanent Genesis ETH reserve backing
90% -> canonical Statics treasury
```

The reserve and treasury percentages are configuration, not a promise that a
future deployment will retain these defaults.

## Draws and reusable capacity

An active, unexpired facility may draw additional principal with
`drawGenesisCredit(genesisId, amount)` up to the 171,000-STATICS limit and the
Vault's currently available backing. Each draw pays the current origination
service fee and does not reset maturity. Read `creditAvailable(genesisId)` and a
fresh `quoteGenesisCredit(amount)` before submission.

Governance may set `creditIncreasesPaused`. That containment switch blocks new
facilities and additional draws but does not block repayment, extension, or
recovery. It cannot retroactively change outstanding principal.

```solidity Current source surface
drawGenesisCredit(uint256 genesisId, uint256 amount)
repayGenesisCredit(uint256 genesisId, uint256 amount)
creditAvailable(uint256 genesisId)
setCreditIncreasesPaused(bool paused)
```

## Lock and beneficial ownership

An active facility makes the Genesis NFT non-transferable, but the borrower
retains beneficial ownership. The owner may continue normal Genesis activation
while credit is active, and opening or repaying credit does not reset activation,
remove rewards, or remove the activation multiplier.

If the Genesis is linked to a PositionNFT, the existing link is respected while
the facility is active. The lock prevents an owner-changing transfer; it does
not move registration or erase the PositionNFT's other ledger items.

## Extension

`quoteGenesisCreditExtension(genesisId)` returns the current native extension
fee and its reserve/treasury split. `extendGenesisCredit(genesisId)` reads that
fee at execution time and purchases another full service term.

The extension fee is a renewal offer, not a term fixed at origination. A future
fee or split change affects only a later extension payment. It does not alter a
term already purchased, repayment, or the recovery grace period. Repayment does
not refund previously paid service fees.

## Repayment and recovery

Anyone may call `repayGenesisCredit(genesisId, amount)` before recovery executes
because repayment only reduces risk and benefits the recorded Operator owner.
Repayment may be partial or complete. A partial payment restores the same amount
of Vault backing, reduces outstanding principal, and makes that capacity
available for a later owner draw while the facility remains active and
unexpired. Full repayment clears the facility and unlocks the NFT. Neither path
resets activation or rewards.

After maturity plus the configured recovery grace period, anyone may call
`recoverGenesisCredit(genesisId)`. Recovery:

1. restores unused credit and the fixed recovery residual;
2. settles direct Genesis rewards through the recovery boundary;
3. removes the recovered Genesis weight and PositionNFT boost;
4. severs only the recovered Genesis/PositionNFT link;
5. preserves the PositionNFT and unrelated position state;
6. returns the Genesis NFT to vault inventory; and
7. pays the configured caller incentive while indexing the remaining residual
   through the existing Genesis reward books.

The fixed recovery residual is 9,000 STATICS. Its caller share is governed, but
must leave a positive Genesis-holder distribution so a permissionless recovery
caller has an incentive without discarding the remaining reward liability.

## SDK and views

The SDK exposes typed builders for `openGenesisCredit`, `drawGenesisCredit`,
`extendGenesisCredit`, amount-bearing `repayGenesisCredit`, and
`recoverGenesisCredit`, plus the credit and availability views. Onchain quotes
remain authoritative; clients should not cache native fees across transactions.
