Genesis is Statics' fixed-supply NFT and reserve-backed access layer. It is a
separate lifecycle from basket creation: all 5,555 Genesis NFTs exist at
deployment, and the Genesis Vault controls which tokens are available for
acquisition or redemption.

## Acquisition and redemption

During the immutable Genesis Epoch, a vault-owned token can be acquired for the
configured STATICS price plus the native acquisition fee. After the epoch,
acquisition also requires the current reserve buy-in. Always call the vault
quote immediately before submitting a transaction; the quote returns both the
STATICS amount and the required native value.

`quoteGenesisRedemption` and `redeemGenesis` return the fixed STATICS payout
and, after the epoch, the token's share of the accounted native reserve. A
redeemed token returns to vault inventory and may later be acquired again.
Native reserve accounting uses the vault's accounted `reserveETH`, not its raw
balance, so forced or accidental ETH is not treated as redeemable reserve.

## Activation and direct rewards

After the governed Genesis integration handoff, an owner registers a Genesis
token once for the permanent reward interval. Registration follows the token
across ordinary owner-changing transfers; it is not the same thing as
activation. A transfer settles the previous owner's attributed rewards and
resets activation to Tier 0 for the new owner.

Activation increases the Genesis multiplier used by the global STATICS reward
weight. It does not change raw stake, withdrawable principal, collateral,
liquidity rewards, or the Genesis token's direct reward weight.

Direct Genesis rewards are a distinct rail from PositionNFT global rewards.
They use the Genesis reward books and may be paid in STATICS and WETH. See
[Genesis rewards](/docs/genesis/rewards) for launch/permanent claims and
lazy accrual.

## PositionNFT linkage

The same owner must hold both NFTs to call `linkGenesis(positionId, genesisId)`;
ERC-721 approvals do not authorize linking or unlinking. While linked, both NFTs
are locked against owner-changing transfers. The relationship is an accounting
link, not a transfer of Genesis registration.

If secured credit is recovered, the protocol severs only the recovered
Genesis-to-Position relationship and clears its boost. The former owner keeps
the PositionNFT, raw stake, pending maturity, claims, and unrelated legs. The
Genesis token returns to vault inventory and can later be acquired through the
ordinary post-epoch path.

## Integration surfaces

Applications normally discover these addresses from the deployment manifest:

| Surface | Responsibility |
| --- | --- |
| `StaticsGenesis` | ERC-721 ownership, metadata, and transfer locks |
| `StaticsGenesisVault` | Acquisition, redemption, reserve accounting, inventory, and secured-credit lifecycle |
| `GenesisActivationRegistry` | Activation tiers, multipliers, and transfer reset |
| `GenesisLaunchDistributor` | Temporary launch fee ingress and launch reward books |
| `StaticsDiamond` | Permanent Genesis registration, linkage, rewards, and recovery |

The public deployment manifest must only list these contracts after the
corresponding deployment is published. Do not combine an unreleased branch ABI
with an older live testnet address set.
