**STATICS Operators** is the public identity of Statics' fixed 5,555-token NFT
collection. Contracts and APIs retain the `Genesis` vocabulary. The collection
has a vault-backed lifecycle separate from basket creation.

The [recorded Robinhood mainnet Genesis launch](https://github.com/EqualFiLabs/statics/blob/master/deployments/robinhood-mainnet-genesis.json)
on chain ID `4663` deployed standalone contracts. Its manifest does **not**
include `StaticsDiamond` or a PositionNFT. The registration, PositionNFT
linkage, and permanent protocol rewards described below require a separate
deployment with matching interfaces; they are not available at the recorded
mainnet Genesis addresses.

## Standalone Genesis launch

### Operator custody and backing

All token IDs exist at construction, but initial custody is split:

| IDs | Count | Initial custodian | Role |
| --- | ---: | --- | --- |
| `1..5000` | 5,000 | `StaticsGenesisVault` | Vault inventory available through the acquisition lifecycle |
| `5001..5555` | 555 | `StaticsTreasuryVesting` | Fully backed protocol allocation released linearly over 60 days |

The 555 vesting-held Operators count as circulating claims from finalization,
so the Vault receives exactly 99,900,000 STATICS of backing before they become
active. The remaining 100,100,000-token treasury allocation follows a separate
Doppler-native 60-day vesting schedule. See [Launch and vesting](/docs/genesis/launch-and-vesting).

### Acquisition and redemption

During the immutable Genesis Epoch, a vault-owned token can be acquired for the
configured STATICS price plus the native acquisition fee. After the epoch,
acquisition also requires the current reserve buy-in. Always call the vault
quote immediately before submitting a transaction; the quote returns both the
STATICS amount and the required native value.

`quoteGenesisRedemption` and `redeemGenesis` return the fixed STATICS payout
and, after the epoch, the token's share of the accounted native reserve. A
redeemed token returns to vault inventory and may later be acquired again.
Native reserve accounting uses the vault's accounted `reserveETH`, not its raw
balance, so forced or accidental ETH is not treated as redeemable reserve.

### Activation and launch rewards

The mainnet manifest records a standalone `GenesisActivationRegistry` and
`GenesisLaunchDistributor`. Activation is distinct from registration in the
later Diamond integration. Launch reward claims use the launch distributor;
they do not require a `StaticsDiamond` address. See
[Genesis rewards](/docs/genesis/rewards) for the separate launch and permanent
reward books.

### Onchain identity

The ERC-721 name is `Statics Operators` and the symbol is `STATOPS`; the
collection-level onchain metadata presents the brand as **STATICS Operators**.
Per-token JSON and SVG artwork are generated fully onchain. Token identity does
not depend on an offchain metadata server.

## Later protocol integration (current source)

Current protocol source describes a governed handoff to `StaticsDiamond` for
permanent registration, direct rewards, and PositionNFT linkage. The
[deployment record](https://github.com/EqualFiLabs/statics/blob/master/deployment.md)
says the permanent Diamond has not been deployed. Use these flows only after
finding a separate deployment record and matching ABI for that integration.

After a matching handoff, an owner registers a Genesis token once for the
permanent reward interval. Registration follows the token across ordinary
owner-changing transfers; it is not the same thing as activation. A transfer
settles the previous owner's attributed rewards and resets activation to Tier
0 for the new owner.

Activation increases the Genesis multiplier used by global STATICS reward
weight. It does not change raw stake, withdrawable principal, collateral,
liquidity rewards, or the Genesis token's direct reward weight. Direct Genesis
rewards are a distinct rail from PositionNFT global rewards and may be paid in
STATICS and WETH.

### PositionNFT linkage

In a deployment with the matching Diamond integration, the same owner must
hold both NFTs to call `linkGenesis(positionId, genesisId)`; ERC-721 approvals
do not authorize linking or unlinking. While linked, both NFTs are locked
against owner-changing transfers. The relationship is an accounting link,
not a transfer of Genesis registration.

If secured credit is recovered after linkage, the protocol severs only the
recovered Genesis-to-Position relationship and clears its boost. The former
owner keeps the PositionNFT, raw stake, pending maturity, claims, and unrelated
legs. The Genesis token returns to vault inventory and can later be acquired
through the ordinary post-epoch path.

## Contract discovery

For the recorded mainnet launch, read addresses from the
[Genesis deployment manifest](https://github.com/EqualFiLabs/statics/blob/master/deployments/robinhood-mainnet-genesis.json)
and use interfaces matching its `launchSourceCommit`:

| Manifest key | Contract | Responsibility |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `staticsToken` | STATICS ERC-20 | Doppler-launched token |
| `operatorsNft` | `StaticsGenesis` | ERC-721 ownership and metadata |
| `genesisVault` | `StaticsGenesisVault` | Acquisition, redemption, reserve accounting, and secured credit |
| `activationRegistry` | `GenesisActivationRegistry` | Activation state |
| `launchDistributor` | `GenesisLaunchDistributor` | Launch reward books |
| `treasuryVesting` | `StaticsTreasuryVesting` | Vesting of 555 Operators |

`StaticsDiamond` is absent from that manifest. Discover its address and ABI
from a separate, matching deployment record before using permanent Genesis
registration, PositionNFT linkage, or Diamond reward calls. Do not combine
current-source interfaces with the older public testnet beta addresses.
