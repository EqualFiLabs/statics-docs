The standalone Genesis launcher established the STATICS token,
STATICS/WETH market, Operators collection, backing, fee ingress, and temporary
reward system independently of the full Diamond deployment.

## Fixed allocation

| Destination or commitment | Amount |
| --- | ---: |
| Fixed STATICS total supply | 1,000,000,000 |
| Six-curve Doppler market inventory | 800,000,000 |
| Doppler-native treasury vesting | 100,100,000 |
| Backing for 555 treasury-vesting Operators | 99,900,000 |
| Total treasury-side allocation | 200,000,000 |

The 99.9M backing is not liquid treasury inventory. It moves through the
bootstrap contract into `StaticsGenesisVault` and backs the 555 Operators held
by treasury vesting. Each circulating Operator represents 180,000 STATICS of
gross backing. IDs still owned by the Vault do not require backing until
acquired.

## Prepare, Launch, Finalize

1. **Prepare** deploys the permanent fee receiver and immutable treasury
   vesting bootstrap, verifies pinned Robinhood dependencies, predicts the
   deterministic token and PoolId, and commits the exact Airlock calldata in a
   confidential artifact.
2. **Launch** calls the canonical Airlock once. It creates the fixed 1B supply,
   establishes one zero-cliff 60-day schedule for 100.1M treasury STATICS,
   passes 800M to the exact six-curve Multicurve initializer, and routes 99.9M
   to the bootstrap contract.
3. **Finalize** creates the 5,555 Operators in two consecutive batches, moves
   99.9M into Vault backing, starts the 555-NFT vesting schedule, binds the
   Vault, fee receiver, activation registry, and launch distributor, then
   proposes two-step ownership to governance.

Finalization is intentionally separate from market creation so every deployed
contract can authenticate the already-live token and pool before binding.

## Two independent 60-day schedules

Treasury STATICS vest from the Launch timestamp inside the Doppler token. Anyone
may trigger `releaseFor(treasury, 0, 0)`, but released assets always go to the
immutable beneficiary.

The 555 Operators vest from the Finalize timestamp in
`StaticsTreasuryVesting`. Anyone may call `releaseGenesis(maxCount)`; releases
are ascending from ID 5001, capped at 50 NFTs per transaction, and always sent
to the configured withdrawal recipient. The recipient admin may rotate that
recipient but cannot change principal, token range, duration, or release assets.

## What the launch hash binds

The reviewed hash is intended to bind chain provenance, fixed and launch
economics, governance, treasury, integrator, confidential salt commitment,
WETH and Doppler module addresses plus runtime hashes, tick spacing, far tick,
all six curves, metadata, Genesis epoch end, predicted token and PoolId, and the
exact Airlock calldata. Keccak collision resistance is a cryptographic
assumption; the contracts verify equality to the approved value.

## Mechanical facts versus market assumptions

The contracts and verification harnesses can establish token supply, inventory,
tick geometry, allocation, vesting, fee mathematics, and deterministic WETH
requirements from a specified pool state. They do not prove an ETH/USD price,
future USD valuation, trading volume, arbitrage participation, or future
liquidity. Those require separate launch-time attestations and economic models.
