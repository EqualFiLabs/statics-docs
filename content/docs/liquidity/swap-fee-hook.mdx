`StaticsSwapFeeHook` charges fees on both realized legs of every registered
Statics protocol-pool swap. Basket canonical pools and general pools both carry
zero native v4 LP fee; the hook is their swap-revenue surface.

:::warning Source versus deployment
This page describes current `statics` master. The recorded Robinhood testnet
deployment exposes the earlier governed-pool ABI and fee configuration. Do not
send the selectors below to that deployment until a matching upgrade is
published.
:::

## Bilateral fees

The hook charges separately against the realized input and output legs. The
launch manifest configures **50 BPS on each leg**. Governance may update both
rates and the split, constrained by:

```text
f_in + f_out <= 200 BPS            // combined rate cap
configurable shares == 9500 BPS       // creator is fixed at 500 BPS
```

The fee **rate** is PoolId-local. Allocation is governed through one global
basket-pool profile and one global general-pool profile.

## Initial six-way allocation

The initial source profiles are:

```text
                        basket pool    general pool
permanent liquidity     1,000 BPS      3,500 BPS
eligible protocol LPs   2,500 BPS      2,500 BPS
basket stakers          2,500 BPS          0 BPS
global Statics stakers  1,500 BPS      1,500 BPS
creator, fixed            500 BPS        500 BPS
treasury                 2,000 BPS      2,000 BPS
total                   10,000 BPS     10,000 BPS
```

The creator share is carved out first and is not governance-configurable. Each
stored profile must total exactly 9,500 BPS so the fixed creator share brings
the result to 10,000 BPS. Treasury receives split dust. LP, basket-staker,
Statics-staker, creator, and treasury liabilities are accounted through the
Diamond; the permanent-liquidity share stays in the hook.

## Fallbacks to POL

If a pool has no activated staked liquidity, its LP share redirects to permanent
liquidity. If basket staking cannot accept the reward asset, that share also
redirects to permanent liquidity. If global Statics staking cannot accept it,
that share redirects to treasury. Creator accrual never falls back.

```text Unavailable-share routing
no activated LP          -> LP share added to POL
basket cannot accept     -> basket-staker share added to POL
Statics cannot accept    -> Statics-staker share added to treasury
```

General pools encode a zero basket-staker share; they do not enter that fallback.

## Compounding

When both pool currencies are available, the hook compounds matched inventory
into its own full-range position during swap settlement. Unmatched amounts
remain pending; anyone may call `compoundPermanentLiquidity` later. Fees earned
by the hook's position are collected back into pending inventory and may be
compounded again.

## Governance controls

Timelocked Diamond governance may update one PoolId's input/output rate with
`setProtocolPoolFeeRate`; the combined rate remains capped at 200 BPS. It may
update the two global allocation profiles with `setBasketFeeAllocation` and
`setGeneralFeeAllocation`. There is no per-pool allocation override in current
source.

:::note
Rate and profile changes affect only future charges. They do not rewrite
accrued creator credits, LP or staker rewards, treasury revenue, or pending POL.
No threshold, volume, liquidity, or oracle rule changes them automatically.
:::
