Public-pool protocol-owned liquidity (POL) is managed through explicit Uniswap v4 PositionManager NFTs. PoolId-specific inventory is backed by PoolManager claims and reserved in the Statics protocol. The `StaticsLiquidityManager` holds protocol positions with fixed custody recipients; the authorized operator can manage positions but cannot redirect principal, refunds, or NFTs.

## General pools begin without POL

A general pool can be created and traded without a liquidity seed. Managed POL is disabled by default. While disabled, its allocation routes to Treasury. The creator can pay the exact governed activation fee during creation or later to enable **future** POL funding. Activation is permanent; it does not move historical Treasury allocations into POL.

After activation, anyone may settle the pool's claim-backed POL share with `settleProtocolPoolPol(poolId, asset, maximumAmount)`. Settlement reserves the released currency for that PoolId. An authorized operator uses the typed `openProtocolPolPosition`, `increaseProtocolPolPosition`, `decreaseProtocolPolPosition`, `collectProtocolPolFees`, and `closeProtocolPolPosition` lifecycle. These are explicit transactions, not swap-callback compounding.

Basket canonical pools arrive in Phase 2 and have their own creator-funded launch seed. Their POL uses the same managed position and custody machinery.

## Native assets and LP fees

For a native ETH pair, POL principal and position proceeds remain native ETH. The manager forwards only the authorized input to Uniswap and returns unused value to protocol custody. Native LP fees earned by a managed POL position are classified as Treasury revenue; they do not become POL principal. User LP NFTs separately earn their native v4 fee through ordinary Uniswap accounting.

## Closure and limitations

Only governance can begin general-pool decommissioning. It stops swaps and the range gauge. The operator then closes each active POL position in bounded transactions. `finalizeGeneralPoolDecommission` requires zero active POL positions before remaining inventory is classified as Treasury revenue. User LP principal and accrued creator or reward claims are not consumed by this process. Governance can use `replaceLiquidityManager` for new positions; existing positions keep their recorded originating manager.

POL accumulation depends on activated allocation and real swap activity. Creating or activating a pool does not guarantee liquidity depth. There is no automatic background keeper, epoch, ramp, or single permanent full-range hook position. See [Public pools](/docs/liquidity/protocol-pools) for creation and [Public hook fees](/docs/liquidity/swap-fee-hook) for allocation.
