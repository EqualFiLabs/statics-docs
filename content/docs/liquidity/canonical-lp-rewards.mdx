A public pool can hold ordinary Uniswap v4 liquidity without enrolling in Statics rewards. A user who opts into the Phase 1 range gauge binds an approved PositionManager NFT to a Statics PositionNFT and transfers the v4 NFT into the `StaticsLiquidityManager` while the managed leg is active. Both full-range and concentrated ranges can participate.

## Two separate earnings rails

- **Native v4 LP fees** belong to the Uniswap position under its creator-selected static LP fee. `collectNativeFees` and `previewNativeLpFees` use the position's ordinary fee accounting.
- **Gauge rewards** are separately funded and accounted by the Statics protocol. They do not replace, duplicate, or promise native LP fees.

Each public-pool gauge has five slots. Slot 0 is reserved for protocol STATICS funded from a permissionless reserve and routed by raw-stake PoolId allocations on an anchored weekly budget. Slots 1–4 accept direct reward funding, subject to asset policy. A creator-selected share of future direct funding can go to valid PoolId allocators; funders receive the protected split in effect when they fund.

A PositionNFT can allocate its raw STATICS stake across PoolIds after the configured cooldown. Allocation and LP range eligibility are different: staking alone does not make a user an LP, and holding a v4 NFT alone does not enroll it in the gauge.

## Managing a position

The typed manager supports providing or attaching liquidity, increasing, decreasing, rebalancing, collecting native fees, and exiting. Native ETH input requires the exact payable budget; ERC-20 pools send no native value. Review the connected account's approvals, current pool state, tick range, maximum inputs, minimum outputs, and deadline before signing.

`claimLpRewards` and allocator-claim paths pay earned rewards. A user can explicitly forfeit an unclaimable LP reward slot to unblock exit. Principal-first exit and independent claim records keep a failed reward-token transfer from trapping v4 principal. Once a gauge is stopped, new activity is restricted but previously accrued claims remain.

## Batches and later-phase paths

Phase 1 also installs atomic typed reward batches for global, LP, and allocator claims. A batch is bounded to 16 claim groups and 64 asset or slot entries; each group retains its own authorization and minimum-received check. Simulate and estimate gas for the complete batch.

Basket borrow-to-liquidity and canonical basket pools are **Phase 2** features. Their pages remain at [Basket liquidity](/docs/liquidity/canonical-pools) and [Basket lending](/docs/lending/overview). They are not prerequisites for Phase 1 public-pool gauges.
