Phase 1 has a separate permissioned Uniswap v4 path for venues whose access and liquidity rules differ from public pools. It uses `StaticsPermissionedSwapFeeHook`, a PoolId-local controller, trusted exact-input router, and non-transferable approved LP positions. Permissioned pools do **not** use the public hook or its managed POL allocation.

## Creation and control

Only protocol governance, through the timelock, creates a permissioned pool. Its creator must authorize the exact initial terms with an EIP-712 or ERC-1271 signature. `quotePermissionedPool(params)` returns the PoolKey, PoolId, normalized price, and authorization digest before `createPermissionedPool(params, creatorAuthorization)` is scheduled. Creation records the creator, controller, native LP fee, tick spacing, and venue agreement hash.

The creator selects a venue controller. The default controller supports operator-managed admission, and another compatible controller may implement different venue rules. Later terms or controller replacement require creator authorization and governance execution; the replacement path installs a compatible halted controller first. A stale nonce, wrong agreement hash, or wrong controller invalidates the authorization.

## Swaps, positions, and fees

External swaps use the trusted exact-input permissioned router and must pass the active controller. Liquidity uses the dedicated `StaticsPermissionedPositionManager`; approved positions are non-transferable. The companion claims contract records owner credits for forced unwind proceeds. Integrators must use the venue's exact hook, router, controller, and position manager from the matching deployment manifest.

The permissioned hook charges a PoolId-local **output** venue fee and does not build POL. Rewardable pairs initially route 80% of that fee to the creator, 10% to Treasury, and 10% to STATICS stakers. A pair with two restricted currencies routes 80% to the creator and 20% to Treasury, without a staker liability. Governance and venue configuration can change terms for future activity; read the active registration and economics before quoting.

## Keep the two pool paths separate

[Public pools](/docs/liquidity/protocol-pools) can become fee-gated open creation and use bilateral hook fees, range gauges, and optional managed POL. Permissioned pools remain governance-created with creator-authorized venue terms and trusted periphery. A public-pool quote, ABI, or address cannot substitute for the permissioned path. See the [Phase 1 launch guide](/docs/start/phase-one-launch) for release status.
