Phase 1 general pools are Statics-hooked Uniswap v4 markets. They can pair two compatible ERC-20s or native ETH with one compatible ERC-20. They are independent of baskets: registration does not make either currency basket backing, Dollar collateral, or a borrowable asset. Basket canonical pools arrive with Phase 2.

:::warning Match the deployment
The [Phase 1 deployment snapshot](/docs/reference/phase-one-deployment) lists the verified protocol address and four public pools. Each had zero active liquidity at verification. This site does not bundle a matching compiled ABI; confirm current pool state, installed selectors, and fee terms before transacting.
:::

## Creation fee and quote

The Robinhood mainnet Phase 1 deployment verified `poolCreationFee()` at **0.01 ETH**. Anyone can create a public pool by paying the exact fee, which is forwarded to Treasury. This fee is separate from basket and PositionNFT creation fees. Before a transaction, read `poolCreationFee()`, call `quotePool(params)`, and use its sorted PoolKey, PoolId, normalized initial price, creation fee, optional managed-POL activation fee, total native fee, and creator-authorization digest. Requote if configuration or pool state changes.

A creator chooses the pair, static native v4 LP fee from 0 through 999,999 pips, tick spacing, initial raw-unit B-per-A price, bilateral hook-fee rate, creator address, and whether to activate managed POL at creation. Each creator-selected hook-fee leg must be at least **10 pips**; the two legs together may not exceed **20,000 pips (200 BPS)**. The launch default is 500 pips per leg, but a creator may choose a lower rate. Dynamic v4 fees are not accepted. PoolId includes the currencies, native LP fee, tick spacing, and hook; another native LP fee or tick spacing makes a distinct market.

Creator authorization uses EIP-712 domain version `5` with EOA or ERC-1271 signatures when required. The signed `CreatePool` fields include `inputFeePips` and `outputFeePips`; an older signature using BPS fields or domain version `4` does not match. A direct creator can create without a signature. Relayed creation requires the creator's authorization; use the quoted digest and current contract interface rather than reusing an old signed intent. `invalidatePoolCreationNonce` cancels an unused authorization.

## Pool creation and liquidity are separate

`createPool` registers and initializes the market; it does not seed liquidity. A market can initially have zero depth. LPs use ordinary Uniswap v4 PositionManager NFTs or opt into the [PositionNFT range gauge](/docs/liquidity/canonical-lp-rewards). The creator-selected native v4 LP fee belongs to LPs through standard Uniswap accounting. Statics' separate [bilateral hook fee](/docs/liquidity/swap-fee-hook) funds protocol allocations.

A native ETH pool retains ETH as native v4 currency for principal, LP fees, managed POL, and swaps. The reward boundary accounts for ETH hook revenue as WETH, so reward selection and claims use the configured WETH identity. See the [native ETH design](https://github.com/EqualFiLabs/statics/blob/master/docs/adr/native-eth-phase-one.md).

## Creator revenue and POL

Public-pool creator revenue receives a fixed 500 BPS of collected bilateral hook fees in each pool currency. `claimCreatorRevenue(poolId, asset, receiver, minReceived)` pays the current configured recipient. Creator succession and a separate fee-recipient setting are available; read `poolCreatorConfiguration(poolId)` before forming a claim. Neither role can decommission a pool or change its hook-fee rate after creation.

Managed POL is **disabled by default** on a general pool. Until activation, its would-be allocation goes to Treasury and does not build a dormant POL position. The creator may activate future funding with the exact governed fee at creation or later. Activation is permanent and does not retroactively move Treasury revenue. An authorized operator manages explicit positions under protocol custody; see [Managed POL](/docs/liquidity/permanent-liquidity).

## Discovery, fees, and closure

Index `ProtocolPoolCreated` and resolve known markets with `protocolPool(poolId)` and `isProtocolPool(poolId)`; there is no unbounded onchain list. Read `defaultProtocolPoolFeeRate()` and `protocolPoolFeeRate(poolId)` for current hook-fee terms, including whether a PoolId override applies.

Governance may begin general-pool decommissioning, which stops swaps and the gauge. Operators close POL positions in bounded transactions; finalization requires no active POL positions. User v4 principal and accrued creator/reward claims are preserved. The creator cannot decommission the pool.
