The **STATICS Genesis launch is live on Robinhood mainnet**. It established the
fixed STATICS supply, the six-curve STATICS/WETH market, the 5,555 Operators
collection, reserve backing, treasury vesting, fee ingress, and launch reward
system documented here.

The broader Statics basket, Dollar, lending, and general-pool suite follows a
separate deployment and governance lifecycle. The earlier public Robinhood
testnet release and newer September full-stack rehearsal are
[separate records](/docs/reference/robinhood-testnet-deployment). Neither is a source of
mainnet Genesis addresses.

## Basket protocol availability

Basket creation has two onchain modes, switched by `creationFee()`:

| `creationFee()` | Who may create |
| --- | --- |
| **Zero** | Diamond owner only |
| **Positive** | Any caller that pays the exact native fee |

There is no separate onchain partner role or asset allowlist. While creation is
owner-only, governance may coordinate selected basket deployments through the
same creation path. When the fee is positive, compatible ERC-20 baskets become
permissionless to create subject to token-behavior and market risk.

Applications must read the active chain's deployed addresses and current
onchain parameters before enabling basket, Dollar, lending, or general-pool
actions. The [Robinhood testnet records](/docs/reference/robinhood-testnet-deployment)
distinguish the two environments; the [testnet onboarding guide](/docs/start/testnet-onboarding)
and vendored ABI/address downloads target the earlier public release. The
rehearsal has its own manifest and does not replace those download bindings.

## Outcomes remain conditional

The EVM fixes supply, allocation, backing rules, vesting, fee mathematics, and
launch geometry. It does not guarantee market outcomes. Trading volume,
rewards, liquidity growth, arbitrage participation, future valuation, and the
USD value of Operator backing depend on real market activity and external
prices.

Additional mint, redeem, pool, and credit paths may create activity when market
conditions support them. Fee shares may compound into permanent
protocol-owned liquidity when inventory matches. Self-backed basket credit and
Statics Dollar minting remain bounded by their configured risk parameters.
