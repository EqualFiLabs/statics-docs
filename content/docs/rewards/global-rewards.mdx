Phase 1 lets a user stake the configured STATICS token in a [PositionNFT](/docs/core/position-nft) and select reward assets. Each asset indexes only across positions that selected it. A reward requires eligible stake **and** actual fees or funding; staking by itself does not create a payout.

## Stake and select assets

`createAndStake` creates a PositionNFT and stakes in one action; `stake` adds to an existing authorized position. `optInRewardAssets` and `optOutRewardAssets` change selection. The current source starts with an active limit of **12 selected assets per position** and an immutable hard ceiling of 64. Read `maxRewardAssetsPerPosition()` and `hardMaxRewardAssetsPerPosition()` from the target deployment rather than hard-coding either.

Initial stake, a new selection, and top-up deltas mature at the next hourly boundary at least 24 hours later, typically a 24–25 hour wait. Existing mature stake stays eligible. A position cannot select an asset immediately before a large fee and capture earlier accrual. `rewardSelection` reports pending and eligible amounts; `rewardSelectionWithTiming` adds the weighted pending start on releases that support that interface.

Stake is withdrawable without a time cooldown, although stake deployed in a later Morpho phase must first be recalled. Withdrawals consume pending stake before mature stake. A full unstake clears selections but preserves earned claims.

## Pool allocations are a separate choice

A position may allocate its **raw** STATICS stake across PoolIds to direct the protocol-funded weekly [range-gauge incentive](/docs/liquidity/canonical-lp-rewards). Positive staking ingress starts or extends the governed allocation cooldown. Existing allocations can be reduced during cooldown, while increases, redirection, or a new destination wait until it expires. A Genesis activation multiplier, available after the Phase 2 Diamond integration, does not increase raw gauge allocation or withdrawable principal.

## Fee routes and claims

Public-pool swaps can assign a configured bilateral hook-fee share to eligible STATICS stakers. For a native ETH pair, WETH is the reward-accounting identity: one WETH opt-in covers WETH and native-ETH hook revenue. Non-swap fees use a **governed** staker/Treasury split; the source default is 90%/10%, and the active value must be read with `nonSwapStakerShareBps()`. If no stake can accept an asset, its fallback destination applies rather than creating an unbacked claim.

`pendingRewards` previews selected-asset claims. `claimRewards` requires PositionNFT authorization, aligned assets, and per-asset minimum received amounts. Phase 1 also supports bounded atomic batches across global, LP, and allocator claims. Claims are pull-based. [Genesis direct rewards](/docs/genesis/rewards) use separate books, and Phase 2 basket rewards are another separate rail.
