Statics has one global staking rail. Users stake the configured staking token
(`STATICS` on testnet) in a PositionNFT and opt into selected reward assets. Each
reward asset indexes rewards only across positions that selected it, so the
denominator stays bounded and honest.

## Staking token

The staking-token address is immutable at initialization. The token must be a
deployed contract and staking transfers must be exact — taxed or
balance-changing staking tokens are rejected.

## Entering stake

| Method | Action |
| --- | --- |
| `createAndStake` | Create a PositionNFT, select initial reward assets, and stake in one call |
| `stake` | Increase an existing authorized position |
| `optInRewardAssets` / `optOutRewardAssets` | Manage the position's reward selections |

There is no staking cooldown, but “withdrawable” means available stake. STATICS
committed as Morpho collateral is unavailable until recalled and must satisfy
Morpho withdrawal/health checks. Liquidation or external loss can also reduce
the position's recorded available stake after synchronization. See [Morpho
credit](/docs/lending/morpho).

## The 24-hour eligibility delay

Initial stake and new selections enter a per-asset pending tranche that matures
at the next hourly boundary **at least 24 hours later** (a 24–25 hour window).
Top-ups merge with existing pending stake using a weighted pending age; they do
not reset the entire tranche to a fresh 24 hours. Mature stake remains eligible
when a position is increased.

Each matured bucket records its activation index, so pending stake cannot capture
historical fees. Fee accrual and position interactions roll the affected asset's
bounded maturity ring before updating its index.

For a top-up to an existing pending tranche, the age credit is proportional to
the old pending amount, capped at 24 hours, and rounded down:

```text
oldCredit      = min(now - pendingStartTime, 24 hours)
weightedCredit = floor(oldPending * oldCredit / (oldPending + added))
newStartTime   = now - weightedCredit
eligibleAt     = ceil((newStartTime + 24 hours) / 1 hour) * 1 hour
```

Hourly rounding and integer math are part of the eligibility rule; do not derive
an exact activation timestamp from a wallet's last deposit time alone.

:::note
This delay is the anti-fee-theft mechanism: a position that selects an asset
right before a large fee distribution accrues only from its activation index
onward, never retroactively.
:::

## Unstaking

`unstake` removes pending stake before eligible stake and requires an exact
outbound transfer. A full unstake clears the selection list while preserving
already settled claims.

## Non-swap fee routing

Most BasketToken and Dollar fees — mint and redemption fees, BasketToken
origination fee reclassification, BasketToken extension payments, repayment
excess, flash-loan excess, recovery penalties, pegged fees, and the global share
of volatile-series fees — enter the same non-swap ledger. Genesis secured-credit
origination, additional-draw, and extension fees are a separate native-fee rail: they split
between Genesis reserve backing and treasury and do not become global Position
staking rewards. For a fee on asset `a` when `eligibleWeight[a] > 0` (stake
multiplied by each position's active reward multiplier):

```text
stakerAmount   = floor(grossFee * 9000 / 10000)   // 90%
treasuryAmount = grossFee - stakerAmount           // 10%
indexDelta[a]  = floor(stakerAmount * 1e27 / eligibleWeight[a])
```

If `eligibleWeight[a] == 0`, no staker liability is created and the entire fee
accrues to treasury. Indexed whole-token value that never crystallizes to a
position routes to treasury only when that asset's eligible stake returns to
zero.

## Claims and treasury

Claims are pull-based, require PositionNFT authorization, and accept a per-asset
minimum received amount. Claim settlement transfers from the global fee
reservation and never reduces basket backing.

Anyone may call `distributeTreasuryFees(asset)`, but the destination is always
the configured treasury — the caller cannot choose a recipient.

See [Genesis rewards](/docs/genesis/rewards) for the separate direct Genesis
reward books, launch/permanent handoff, and dual-asset batch claims.
